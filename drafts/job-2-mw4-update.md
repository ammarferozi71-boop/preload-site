# TITLE
Modern Warfare 4 PC Requirements, Preload Time and Install Size

# SLUG

MW4 PC Requirements, Preload Time and Unlock Time | Preload

# META DESCRIPTION
Activision's PC specs for Modern Warfare 4: 115 GB SSD, TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot. PC preload opens Oct 15 at 9 AM PT; PC launch is Oct 23, midnight local time.

# ARTICLE BODY
**Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 launches on October 23, 2026, and on PC Activision says the full game releases "at midnight local time."** [e16] [e7] Players who pre-purchase digitally on PC can preload from **October 15 at 9 AM PT**, and the PC version needs **115 GB on an SSD** with **TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot** turned on. [e8] [e13] [e14] Every game fact on this page comes from Activision's own Call of Duty posts, read on October 12, 2026; the time-zone table and the daylight saving notes are our own conversion of Activision's 9 AM PT time.

## Key dates

| What | When | Who it applies to |
|---|---|---|
| PC preload opens | Thursday, October 15, 9 AM PT | Digital pre-purchases on PC [e8] |
| Campaign Early Access | Friday, October 16 (start hour not given in the post read) | Digital pre-orders of the Standard Digital or Vault Edition [e17] |
| Full launch on PC | Friday, October 23, at midnight local time | PC (Activision's PC post) [e7] |

The game launches on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC, and on PC it is sold on Steam, Battle.net and Xbox on PC. [e16] [e9] Activision says it will not be on Game Pass at launch. [e16]

## Launch time on October 23

Activision's PC post says the full game releases on October 23 at midnight local time; the Activision posts read for this page do not give unlock times for each time zone. [e7] The Activision posts read for this page do not give a console unlock time, so check your console's store listing if you play there. [e7] [e16]

Campaign Early Access starts on Friday, October 16, but the Activision post read for this page does not give a start hour, and it notes that "actual play time" is subject to "applicable time zone differences." [e17]

## PC system requirements

Activision published three PC tiers. The recommended tier targets 90 FPS in multiplayer on High settings, and the top tier targets High ray tracing at 4K upscaled. [e11] [e12]

| | Minimum | Recommended | Ultra ray tracing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OS** | Windows 10 64-bit (latest update) [e10] | Windows 11 64-bit (latest update) [e11] | Windows 11 64-bit [e12] |
| **CPU** | Ryzen 5 1400 or Core i5-6600 [e10] | Ryzen 5 3600 or Core i7-8700K [e11] | Ryzen 7 3700X or Core i7-10700K [e12] |
| **GPU** | RX 470, GTX 970 / 1060 or Arc A580 [e10] | RX 6600 XT, RTX 3060 Ti or Arc B580 [e11] | RX 9070 XT or RTX 5070 Ti [e12] |

| **Storage** | 115 GB, SSD required [e10] | 115 GB, SSD required [e11] | 115 GB, SSD required [e12] |

Activision says the specs are valid for launch and may be updated later. [e13]

## TPM 2.0, Secure Boot and AVX are required

Activision states that TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot are required to play Modern Warfare 4 on PC, and that only Intel and AMD processors with the AVX instruction set are supported at this time. [e14] If you have never checked these settings, our guide shows [how to check TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot on Windows](/guides/check-tpm-secure-boot-windows).

## Install size and storage

All three PC tiers list 115 GB on an SSD. [e13] Activision adds that mandatory game updates may need more storage space, so leave room beyond 115 GB. [e13] If your drive is nearly full, see [how to free up SSD space for large games](/guides/free-up-ssd-space-for-large-games). The Activision posts read for this page do not give console install sizes. [e16]

## Checklist before preload and launch

- To preload, pre-purchase digitally on PC; the PC game is sold on Steam, Battle.net and Xbox on PC. [e8] [e9]
- Turn on TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot, and confirm your Intel or AMD CPU supports AVX. [e14]
- Clear more than 115 GB on an SSD. [e13]
- Compare your graphics card and RAM with the tier you want in the table above. [e10] [e11]
- Campaign Early Access on October 16 is for digital pre-orders of the Standard Digital or Vault Edition. [e17]

New to preloading? Read [how preloading works on Steam, Xbox, PlayStation and Battle.net](/install/how-game-preloading-works).

## PC features Activision lists

- Advanced Shader Delivery, which sends precompiled shaders ahead of time so the game launches faster [e15]
- HDR10+ support [e15]

## Quick answers

**When does Modern Warfare 4 unlock on PC?**
Activision's PC post says the full game releases on October 23 at midnight local time. [e7]

**When can I preload Modern Warfare 4 on PC?**
From October 15 at 9 AM PT, which is noon Eastern, for digital pre-purchases. [e8]

**How big is Modern Warfare 4 on PC?**
115 GB, and it must go on an SSD; updates may need more space. [e13]

**Is Modern Warfare 4 on Game Pass?**
Not at launch, according to Activision. [e16]

**Does Modern Warfare 4 need TPM 2.0?**
Yes. TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot are both required on PC. [e14]
